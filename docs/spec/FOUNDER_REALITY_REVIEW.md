# Founder Reality Review

Track: Internal Workflow Packaging

This review defines how C-Suite Command validates the Roles & Responsibility
Map before it is presented to the founder.

## Review objective

Confirm that the role map reflects how the founder actually operates today, with
custom GPT support layered around the founder rather than replacing the
founder.

## Founder-reality checks

### Check 1: No invented staffed team

- The workflow must not imply separate staffed departments where the founder is
  still the operator.
- GPT owners may own stages in the packet, but the founder remains the human
  authority over approval and execution.

### Check 2: Product & Ops is truly the conductor

- Product & Ops (Launch HQ) must initiate the workflow.
- Product & Ops must also package the final founder-review-ready packet.
- Other GPTs may shape or validate content, but they do not replace the
  conductor role.

### Check 3: Systems & Architecture stays canonical

- Systems & Architecture must define the role-map structure before final
  assembly.
- The canonical spec must constrain the workflow instead of being retrofitted
  after the map is written.

### Check 4: C-Suite Command validates reality, not aspiration

- Review comments must remove fake delegation and future-state assumptions.
- The review must call out where the founder is still the actual decision maker.

### Check 5: Contracts & Governance remains optional

- Governance normalization may refine wording only.
- It must not become a mandatory blocker for founder review.

## Review findings for v1

- The workflow is grounded in a founder-led operating model.
- GPT ownership is explicit at each stage rather than implied.
- The packet ends in founder review instead of pretending execution is fully
  delegated.
- The optional governance pass is properly constrained to normalization only.
- OpsToys remains the browser-tool label and Custos remains part of Scientia.

## Required founder checkpoints

- Confirm the final role inventory matches current reality.
- Confirm the handoff chain is how the founder wants the GPTs orchestrated.
- Confirm no stage implies a staffed operator that does not exist.
- Confirm the packaged map is sufficient for near-term execution.

## Approval recommendation

Recommend founder review of the packet as drafted.

No additional architecture is required for this ticket.

If a future revision adds automation, staffing layers, or execution tooling
changes, that work should be treated as a separate task rather than folded into
this packet.
