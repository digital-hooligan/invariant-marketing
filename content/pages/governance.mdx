Decisions should be explainable.

Governance is not about automation or control systems.
It is about ensuring that important decisions remain connected to evidence, context, and accountability.
The Scientia operating model introduces decision-centric governance.

## The Governance Problem

Many organizations face the same governance gap:

- decisions appear without clear evidence
- reasoning is buried in scattered discussions
- institutional memory fades with team turnover
- accountability becomes unclear

Without structured governance, decisions become difficult to review or learn from.

## Evidence Trails

Governance begins with evidence.
Signals captured through the system remain linked to decisions.
This allows teams to trace:

- what triggered a decision
- which signals influenced it
- what information was considered

Solum supports evidence capture and traceability within the system.

## Context Continuity

Evidence alone is not enough.
Decisions must also reflect the surrounding context.
Pondus maintains contextual continuity, preserving:

- environmental conditions
- historical references
- related signals
- past decisions

This ensures decisions remain understandable months or years later.

## Decision Accountability

Decisions should be visible and attributable.
RadixOS structures decision governance and accountability, ensuring decisions are connected to:

- responsible actors
- supporting context
- associated evidence

Governance emerges from structured transparency, not automation.

## Organizational Outcome

With structured governance:

- decisions remain explainable
- evidence stays attached to outcomes
- review cycles become possible
- organizations build durable institutional memory

Governance becomes a property of the system rather than a manual process.
