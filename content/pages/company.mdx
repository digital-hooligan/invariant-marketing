The studio and legal entity behind the work.

Digital Hooligan LLC is the legal company behind Invariant.
Invariant is the studio identity used for public marketing, systems work, and
client-facing engagements.

## Digital Hooligan LLC

Digital Hooligan LLC:

- contracts and operates the studio work
- owns the intellectual property developed through that work
- provides the legal and operational foundation for engagements

## Invariant

Invariant is the studio identity through which the work is developed and
published. The studio focuses on:

- applied systems design
- decision architecture
- workflow implementation
- operational experimentation

## Direction

The goal of the work is to help organizations build systems where decisions stay
understandable, traceable, and accountable over time.
